Why TabPFN Foundation Model? (Benchmark on Small Medical Data)

Diabetic patient logs are small tabular records (60–100 rows). Traditional neural nets and tree models overfit heavily on small samples. TabPFN was pretrained on millions of synthetic tabular datasets to perform instant, Bayesian in-context inference without training loops or hyperparameter tuning.

The In-Context Learning Advantage

TabPFN treats the patient’s past 90 days as a single prompt context inside its transformer attention heads. It estimates the entire posterior distribution over crash probabilities in a single forward pass, providing perfectly calibrated uncertainties that prevent false alarms while catching edge-case crashes.
